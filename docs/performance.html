Tradeoff 2: JSON-field indexing vs typed columns, and storage growth with every version kept

The one body index is an expression GIN on bytea decoded as jsonb, limited to invitations by its predicate. That read filters with @>, and organization_id is the GIN index condition. What is missing is a join on a body field. The backend asserts BODY_INDEXED_PATH !== '/invitations/' and throws otherwise, because "a generic plan would not use the index" (api/backend-postgres.ts). Adding a second index needs DDL, and DDL arrives only by wipe and reseed. Every version is kept as two full wire messages plus digests. Bytes per pair, index bytes, and growth are unmeasured. TODO.md's "Growth" item plans to measure them.

Tradeoff 4: does the 412 from the unique index hold up under real contention?

Correctness, yes: tests/pg-races.test.ts shows one winner and one 412, and a blind write retries up to three succession races before answering 409 (API.md). Throughput is not covered. Beyond one document, writes serialize on the organization genesis row (FOR SHARE / FOR UPDATE), so a busy organization's roster writes queue behind one row. Its effect under load is unmeasured. Production runs a single mint process (ARCHITECTURE.md), and multi-replica writing is item 13.
